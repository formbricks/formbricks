Link Surveys make it easy for your users to give you feedback without interrupting their workflow. Each one has a shareable URL, so respondents need nothing but a browser. This quickstart has your agent create one and shares its link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and connect your agent">
    Create a free Formbricks Cloud account at [app.formbricks.com](https://app.formbricks.com/auth/signup), or [self-host](/self-hosting/overview) Formbricks. Then connect Claude, Codex or Cursor. [Getting started](/platform/getting-started) walks through it in a few minutes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask for a survey">
    Describe the survey you want, for example: *"Create a link survey in my workspace that asks how likely people are to recommend us (0 to 10) and why."* The agent drafts the survey document with `generate_survey` or writes it directly, checks it with `validate_survey`, and saves it with `create_survey`. New surveys start as drafts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tune the settings">
    Ask for what the survey needs: when it runs or closes, what happens at the end, hidden fields, multiple languages, custom CSS. Everything a survey can do lives in the survey document, and the agent reads the field reference of the [v3 API](/api-v3-reference/introduction) through the same tools.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Ask the agent to publish the survey. Its link is your instance's public survey URL followed by `/s/<surveyId>`, for example `https://app.formbricks.com/s/<surveyId>`. Share it by email, chat or QR code, [embed it](/surveys/link-surveys/embed-surveys) in a web page, and add [URL parameters](/surveys/link-surveys/data-prefilling) to prefill answers or [track the source](/surveys/link-surveys/source-tracking).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the responses">
    Ask the agent to count and read the responses (`count_responses`, `list_responses`), summarize them, or tag and group them. Responses can also be sent to your own systems with a [webhook](/platform/webhooks).
  </Step>
</Steps>
