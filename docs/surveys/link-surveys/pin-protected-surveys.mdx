A PIN keeps a link survey from being answered by anyone who happens to have the URL. The link still works; without the four digits it goes no further than a prompt.

## Enabling PIN protection

Open the survey editor, go to the **Settings** tab, and expand **Response Options**. Switch on **Protect survey with a PIN** and type the four digits respondents will need.

![The PIN row of Response Options](/images/surveys/link-surveys/pin-protected-surveys/response-option.webp)

The PIN can be changed at any time from the same place. Changing it locks out anyone still holding the old one.

## What the respondent sees

Opening the link shows the prompt instead of the first question.

![The PIN prompt a respondent sees](/images/surveys/link-surveys/pin-protected-surveys/pin-prompt.webp)

A wrong PIN is rejected and can be retried; the right one opens the survey as usual.

## When to use it

- Confidential surveys — internal company feedback, or research where the questions themselves are sensitive.

- Surveys for one event or one group, where you can hand the PIN out in the room.

- Surveys where the link is likely to be forwarded and you would rather it went no further.

<Note>
  A PIN gates access, it does not identify anyone. Everyone who has it shares it. If you need to know
  *which* person answered, use [personal links](/surveys/link-surveys/personal-links) or
  [single-use links](/surveys/link-surveys/single-use-links) instead.
</Note>
