Requiring a verified email address before someone can answer keeps anonymous and throwaway responses out of your data, and attaches a real address to every response you do collect.

## Enabling email verification

Open the survey editor, go to the **Settings** tab, and expand **Response Options**. Switch on **Verify email before submission**.

![The email verification row of Response Options](/images/surveys/link-surveys/verify-email-before-survey/response-option.webp)

<Note>
  This is a link survey feature. App surveys already know who the respondent is.
</Note>

## What the respondent sees

Opening the link asks for an address first. Formbricks sends a link to that address, and the survey opens once it is followed.

![The email gate a respondent sees](/images/surveys/link-surveys/verify-email-before-survey/email-gate.webp)

**Preview survey questions** lets someone read the survey without verifying anything. They cannot answer — it is there so a cautious respondent can see what they are being asked before handing over an address.

## Where the verified address shows up

Each response carries the address it was verified against, visible on the response in the Responses tab and included in exports.

## When to use it

- Market research that has to come from a known population.

- Surveys behind gated content, where the audience is a list you already hold.

- Academic or professional studies where a response has to be attributable.

<Note>
  Verification needs a working SMTP configuration. On a self-hosted instance, see [SMTP
  Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration/smtp).
</Note>
