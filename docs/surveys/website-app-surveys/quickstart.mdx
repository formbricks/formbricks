---
title: "Setup Web & Mobile SDK connection"
description: "Connect your website or app to Formbricks with the SDK, then have your agent create a survey that appears inside it."
icon: "rocket"
sidebarTitle: "SDK Setup"
---

App surveys run inside your website or mobile app. You add the SDK once; surveys are created and changed by your agent, with no further code changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your workspace ID">
    Open **Organization → Workspaces** in the Formbricks account shell and copy the **ID** of the workspace the surveys belong to. Your agent can also read it with `list_workspaces` over [MCP](/platform/mcp/overview).

    The SDK needs two values: the **workspace ID** and the **app URL** of your Formbricks instance (`https://app.formbricks.com` on Cloud, your own domain when you self-host).

    <Note>
      Not the person who will add the SDK? Send them the two values, or invite them to the organization under **Organization → Members**.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the Formbricks SDK to your app">
    Pick the method that matches your stack and follow its guide. Each snippet takes the workspace ID and app URL from the previous step.

    | Method | Covers |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | **Website** | HTML, React.js, Next.js, Vue.js |
    | **Mobile** | React Native, Swift, Android, Flutter |
    | **Google Tag Manager** | Any website, without touching its code |

    See the [framework guides](/surveys/website-app-surveys/framework-guides) for code and [Google Tag Manager](/surveys/website-app-surveys/google-tag-manager) for the no-code route. For plain HTML, paste the snippet into the `<head>` of every page of your website.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Open the website or launch the app you added the SDK to. If surveys do not show up later, turn on [Debug Mode](/surveys/website-app-surveys/framework-guides#activate-debug-mode) and read your browser console. On mobile, check your platform's log output instead: the Xcode console, Logcat, or your Metro or Flutter console.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask your agent for a survey">
    [Connect your agent](/platform/getting-started), then ask it to create a **website & app survey** in the workspace, triggered on an existing action such as a page view, and to publish it. `list_action_classes` shows the actions the workspace has; the survey references one as `distribution.triggers[].actionClassId`. The survey appears whenever the Formbricks widget detects that action.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how often it shows">
    A common hiccup is users seeing one survey but it doesn't appear again. This is due to the defaults we chose to avoid survey fatigue: a survey is shown once per user, and a Cooldown Period applies across surveys. While testing, ask your agent to ignore the Cooldown Period and keep showing the survey while conditions match, and change this after testing to avoid user fatigue. [Survey Display Logic](/surveys/website-app-surveys/survey-display-logic) explains every gate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Understand caching behavior">
    For better scalability, we cache the request the SDK makes to the server. This allows you to use Formbricks on websites with millions of visitors without high hosting cost.

    <Note>
      **Important**: Any changes to surveys, action classes, or workspace settings will take **up to 1 minute** to reflect in your app/website due to server-side caching. This includes survey modifications, new triggers, styling changes, and other updates.
    </Note>

    For troubleshooting, you can enable [Debug Mode](/surveys/website-app-surveys/framework-guides#activate-debug-mode) to see detailed logs in your browser console.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check browser compatibility">
    Formbricks is designed to work with all modern browsers. Please ensure your users are using a supported version:

    | Browser | Minimum Version | Release Date |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Chrome** | 111+ | March 2023 |
    | **Edge** | 111+ | March 2023 |
    | **Firefox** | 128+ | July 2024 |
    | **Safari** | 16.4+ | March 2023 |

    *Internet Explorer is not supported.*
  </Step>
</Steps>
