Every creative counts how often it was shown and clicked. Open a creative from the **Creatives** list to see its summary page, and find the responses it brought in on the survey it opens.

<Note>
  Creatives are part of the Formbricks [Enterprise Edition](/self-hosting/advanced/license), on self-hosted
  instances whose license includes Creatives. They are not available on Formbricks Cloud.
</Note>

## The summary page

![The summary page of a feedback button with impressions, clicks, click rate, daily totals and pages](/images/surveys/creatives/measure-performance/summary.webp)

The header shows what the creative opens and its schedule, with the status menu, **Refresh** and **Edit** next to it. Below it:

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Impressions** | Page views on which the creative showed. Showing it again on the same page doesn't add one; a new page, including a route change in a single-page app, does. A creative counts as shown once it is on the page, even if the visitor didn't scroll to it. |
| **Clicks** | Presses that opened its survey or link. Closing a banner isn't a click, nor is following a link in a banner's text, nor pressing the creative while its survey is already open. |
| **Click rate** | Clicks divided by impressions. |

**Daily totals** charts impressions and clicks per day. **Pages** lists the 100 pages with the most impressions, each with its impressions, clicks and click rate, and adds up all other pages in one row. A page is the host and path, such as `www.acme-bank.com/loans/mortgages`, without the query string or `#hash`. Each UTC day, a creative counts its first 1,000 different pages one by one and any further pages together, so on a very large site a busy page reached late in the day can land in the all-other-pages row.

### Date range

Pick a range above the numbers: **Last 30 days** by default, from **Today** to **Last year**, or a **Custom range** of up to 366 days. Days are counted in UTC, and the numbers are usually up to a minute behind; on instances with thousands of busy creatives they can lag a little longer.

### What isn't counted

- Bots and crawlers.
- Anything while your license doesn't include Creatives, since creatives aren't shown then.

Deleting a creative deletes its numbers. Archiving it keeps them, and duplicating it starts the copy from zero.

<Note>
  **Self-hosting:** impressions and clicks are counted in Redis and written to the database every minute by
  the background job worker, which runs by default. Without a running worker, counts not yet written expire
  after about two days and are lost; counts already written stay on the summary page. See [Job
  Runner](/self-hosting/configuration/job-runner).
</Note>

## Responses opened by a creative

Every response to a survey opened by a creative records which creative opened it, as **Opened by creative** (the creative's name when the response came in) and **Opened by creative ID**, as long as that creative still exists and still opens the survey when the response arrives:

- on the response card, with the rest of the response's [metadata](/surveys/general-features/metadata);
- as a column in the response table, shown by default on surveys that creatives open;
- in the response **Filter**, so you can compare what the feedback button brought in with the survey's own popups;
- in the CSV and Excel export.

![The response table with the Opened by creative column](/images/surveys/creatives/measure-performance/opened-by-creative.webp)

This works for app surveys opened as a popup and for link surveys opened in a new tab. Responses keep the creative's name after the creative is renamed or deleted. Both fields are [reserved fields](/surveys/embedded-data/reserved-fields), so they can't be used as names of your own embedded data.

## Counts in the API

The [Management API v3](/api-v3-reference/introduction) returns the same counts with `GET /api/v3/creatives/{creativeId}/stats`, per UTC day, and broken down by page or by device type (desktop, tablet, phone) with `groupBy`. The MCP tool `get_creative_stats` reads them too.
