<Note>
  Survey visibility is part of the [Enterprise Edition](/self-hosting/advanced/license). On Formbricks Cloud
  it comes with plans that include access control.
</Note>

## Two options

| | **Restricted** | **Visible to {Workspace}** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who can see it | The author, plus your organization's owners and managers | Everyone in the workspace |
| Collects responses | Yes | Yes |
| Sends to follow-ups, integrations, webhooks, workflows and feedback sources | No | Yes |

New surveys start **Restricted**. Respondents are never affected: a restricted survey still works through its
link, your website or your app.

## Change visibility

- **When activating:** Formbricks asks _Who can view this survey in your workspace?_ before it is activated.
- **Anytime:** open **Collaborate** from the survey's row menu or the editor.

Only the author and organization owners or managers can change it.

## Restricting a survey

Formbricks shows how many colleagues lose access and asks you to confirm.

<Warning>
  A survey connected to an integration, webhook, workflow or feedback source can't be restricted. Disconnect
  those first — Formbricks lists them for you.
</Warning>

## If the author leaves

Their restricted surveys stay restricted. Owners and managers can still open them and make them visible to
the workspace.
