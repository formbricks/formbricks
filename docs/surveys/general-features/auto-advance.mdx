title: Auto-advance
description: Move respondents to the next question as soon as they answer, without clicking Next.

---

With auto-advance on, a respondent moves to the next question the moment they pick an answer. They don't have to click **Next**.

To turn it on, open your survey's **Settings** tab, go to **Response Options** and switch on **Auto-advance after one answer**. It's off by default.

## Which questions auto-advance

Auto-advance works on these question types:

- Rating
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- CSAT
- CES
- Single-select
- Picture selection, when only one picture can be chosen

It only applies to a card that holds a single question. A card with two or more questions always shows **Next**, so respondents can answer all of them first.

## What respondents see

- On a required question, the **Next** button is hidden: picking an answer moves them on.
- On an optional question, **Next** stays visible, so they can skip it.
- On a single-select question, picking **Other** shows **Next** again, so they can type their answer before moving on.
- The **Back** button still works, unless you [hide it](/surveys/general-features/hide-back-button).
