<Warning>
  Email Follow-ups are deprecated in favor of [Workflows](/workflows/overview). The announced removal
  date is **1 December 2026** (UTC calendar date). This is not evidence of a midnight shutdown on
  every self-hosted installation; behavior depends on the installed release.
</Warning>

## Move to Workflows

1. Confirm Workflows is available in your Cloud plan or self-hosted Enterprise license and release.
2. Rebuild each follow-up using **Response completed**, its ending-card filter and **Send email**.
3. Copy recipients, reply-to, subject, content and response-data settings. Test with a sample response.
4. Disable the old follow-up when enabling its replacement to avoid duplicate email delivery.

Workflows is in Beta. Check [current capabilities](/workflows/overview) before switching.
Existing follow-ups keep their editor tab. Self-hosted Community installations can still have follow-ups
without Workflows entitlement; confirm the replacement path with your support contact before removing them.
The published announcement does not establish a removal release for those installations.

## What are Email Follow-ups?

Email followups allow you to automatically send customized emails to respondents based on their survey responses or when they reach specific survey endings. This feature is perfect for:
- Sending thank you notes
- Following up with respondents
- Providing additional information
- Sharing survey response data

### Trigger Types

<Card title="Response-based">
  Emails are sent when a response to your survey is completed.
</Card>

<Card title="Ending-based">
  Emails are triggered when respondents reach specific survey endings.
</Card>

## Setting Up Email Follow-ups

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Follow-ups tab">
    It sits at the end of the survey editor's tab row, after Settings. `New follow-up` opens the dialog where the rest of these steps happen.

    <Image src="/images/surveys/general-features/email-followups/followups-tab.webp" alt="The Follow-ups tab of the survey editor" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Recipients">
    The "To" field can be configured to use:

    <ul>
      <li><strong>Verified Email:</strong> The email address entered in the email verification step (when email verification is enabled)</li>
      <li><strong>Email Questions:</strong> Responses to question type `Open Text` of type `email`</li>
      <li><strong>Contact Info:</strong> Responses to question type `Contact`</li>
      <li><strong>Hidden Fields:</strong> Values from hidden fields</li>
      <li><strong>Team Members:</strong> Members of your team</li>
      <li><strong>Yourself:</strong> Your own email address</li>
    </ul>

    <Image src="/images/surveys/general-features/email-followups/new-followup.webp" alt="The Create a new follow-up dialog" />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Reply-To">
    - Add one or more valid email addresses
    - Addresses can be added by typing and pressing space or comma
    - Invalid email addresses are automatically rejected
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Email Content">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Subject:</strong> Customize your email subject line</li>
      <li><strong>Body:</strong> Supports basic HTML formatting (`p`, `span`, `b`, `strong`, `i`, `em`, `a`, `br` tags)</li>
      <li>
        <strong>Survey Response Data:</strong> Option to include detailed response data with support for:
        <ul>
          <li>File uploads</li>
          <li>Images</li>
          <li>Rankings</li>
          <li>Translations</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save to Activate">
    Once you've configured all settings, save your survey to activate the email follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>
