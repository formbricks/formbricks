The Interview Prompt picks a specific group of users — power users, say — and invites them to a call, in the product rather than by email.

## Purpose

An email invite competes with everything else in an inbox. A prompt shown while someone is already using the product reaches them when your product is what they are thinking about.

## 1. Start from the template

Open **Surveys**, click **New Survey** and choose **Choose a template**. Search for **Interview Prompt**, then click the card to create the survey.

![The Interview Prompt template in the template picker](/images/surveys/best-practices/interview-prompt/template.webp)

The template is a short call-to-action with a link to your scheduler. Point it at your own booking link before publishing.

## 2. Trigger it at the right moment

Who sees this matters more than when, so most of the work is in **Target Audience** — see [attribute-based targeting](/surveys/website-app-surveys/attribute-based-targeting) for building the segment. The trigger can then be something as broad as opening the dashboard, because the segment has already narrowed the audience.

Open the **Settings** tab, find **Survey Trigger** and add the action.

![The Survey Trigger section with the action attached](/images/surveys/best-practices/interview-prompt/trigger.webp)

<Tip>
  Creating the action itself — Click versus Page View, Inner Text versus CSS Selector — is the same dialog
  in every recipe. It is walked through once on the [Feedback Box](/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box)
  page, and the full reference is the [Actions manual](/surveys/website-app-surveys/actions).
</Tip>

## 3. Decide how often it may appear

Still on **Settings**, open **Visibility & Recontact**. Choose **Show only once**. Someone who has already declined an interview should not be asked again every time they open the app.

<Note>
  Recontact Options and the workspace-wide [Cooldown Period](/surveys/website-app-surveys/cooldown-period)
  are two separate gates and a survey has to pass both — Recontact Options first, then the cooldown. If
  your workspace shows at most one survey a week, this survey inherits that unless you set **Ignore
  Cooldown Period**.
</Note>

## 4. Publish

**Publish** puts it live. The widget picks up the change within a minute.

<Note>
  This is an app survey, so it needs the Formbricks widget running in your product. If you have not
  installed it yet, follow [the quickstart](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart) first.
</Note>
