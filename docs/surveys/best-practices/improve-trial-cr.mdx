This survey asks people who are ending a trial what stopped them from paying.

## Purpose

Trial conversion analytics tell you how many people dropped and where. They cannot tell you why, and the why is the only part you can act on.

## 1. Start from the template

Open **Surveys**, click **New Survey** and choose **Choose a template**. Search for **Trial Conversion**, then click the card to create the survey.

![The Trial Conversion template in the template picker](/images/surveys/best-practices/improve-trial-cr/template.webp)

The template asks what stopped them and what would have changed their mind. Both are open text on purpose — a multiple choice here only returns the options you already thought of.

## 2. Trigger it at the right moment

Two triggers work, depending on how your trial ends. If cancelling is a button, match it with **Inner Text** `Cancel Trial`. If it is a page such as `/trial-cancelled`, use a **Page View** action with **Limit to specific pages**.

Open the **Settings** tab, find **Survey Trigger** and add the action.

![The Survey Trigger section with the action attached](/images/surveys/best-practices/improve-trial-cr/trigger.webp)

<Tip>
  Creating the action itself — Click versus Page View, Inner Text versus CSS Selector — is the same dialog
  in every recipe. It is walked through once on the [Feedback Box](/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box)
  page, and the full reference is the [Actions manual](/surveys/website-app-surveys/actions).
</Tip>

## 3. Decide how often it may appear

Still on **Settings**, open **Visibility & Recontact**. Choose **Show only once**.

<Note>
  Recontact Options and the workspace-wide [Cooldown Period](/surveys/website-app-surveys/cooldown-period)
  are two separate gates and a survey has to pass both — Recontact Options first, then the cooldown. If
  your workspace shows at most one survey a week, this survey inherits that unless you set **Ignore
  Cooldown Period**.
</Note>

## 4. Publish

**Publish** puts it live. The widget picks up the change within a minute.

<Note>
  This is an app survey, so it needs the Formbricks widget running in your product. If you have not
  installed it yet, follow [the quickstart](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart) first.
</Note>
