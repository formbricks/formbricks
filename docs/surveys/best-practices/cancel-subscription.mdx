A churn survey asks why someone is leaving, at the one moment they are certain of the answer.

## Purpose

Asked a week later by email, the answer is a rationalisation. Asked at the click of the cancel button, it is the reason.

## 1. Start from the template

Open **Surveys**, click **New Survey** and choose **Choose a template**. Search for **Churn Survey**, then click the card to create the survey.

![The Churn Survey template in the template picker](/images/surveys/best-practices/cancel-subscription/template.webp)

The template asks for a reason and then for detail. Keep it to that: someone mid-cancellation will not fill in a long form.

## 2. Trigger it at the right moment

The trigger is your cancel button. Open the **Settings** tab, find **Survey Trigger** and add an action that matches it — **Inner Text** `Cancel Subscription` is usually enough, and a **CSS Selector** such as `#cancel-subscription` is better if more than one button carries that label.

![The Survey Trigger section with the action attached](/images/surveys/best-practices/cancel-subscription/trigger.webp)

<Tip>
  Creating the action itself — Click versus Page View, Inner Text versus CSS Selector — is the same dialog
  in every recipe. It is walked through once on the [Feedback Box](/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box)
  page, and the full reference is the [Actions manual](/surveys/website-app-surveys/actions).
</Tip>

## 3. Decide how often it may appear

Still on **Settings**, open **Visibility & Recontact**. Choose **Show only once**. A cancellation is a one-time event, and someone who has already answered should not be asked twice.

<Note>
  Recontact Options and the workspace-wide [Cooldown Period](/surveys/website-app-surveys/cooldown-period)
  are two separate gates and a survey has to pass both — Recontact Options first, then the cooldown. If
  your workspace shows at most one survey a week, this survey inherits that unless you set **Ignore
  Cooldown Period**.
</Note>

## 4. Publish

**Publish** puts it live. The widget picks up the change within a minute.

<Note>
  This is an app survey, so it needs the Formbricks widget running in your product. If you have not
  installed it yet, follow [the quickstart](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart) first.
</Note>
