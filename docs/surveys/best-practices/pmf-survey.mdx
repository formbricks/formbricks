The Superhuman product-market-fit survey asks how disappointed someone would be if they could no longer use your product. Above 40% answering "very disappointed" is the usual signal of fit.

## Purpose

The number is only meaningful if you ask people who have actually used the product. Asking everyone, including people who signed up yesterday, produces a number that measures your signup flow rather than your product.

## 1. Start from the template

Open **Surveys**, click **New Survey** and choose **Choose a template**. Search for **Product Market Fit**, then click the card to create the survey.

![The Product Market Fit template in the template picker](/images/surveys/best-practices/pmf-survey/template.webp)

The template is the Superhuman question set. Do not reword the first question — the 40% benchmark only means anything against the standard wording.

## 2. Trigger it at the right moment

Ask people who have used the product enough to have an opinion. That is easiest as a **code action** fired from your own app once a user reaches, say, their third session — a page view cannot tell you that.

Open the **Settings** tab, find **Survey Trigger** and add the action. See [Code Actions](/surveys/website-app-surveys/actions#setting-up-code-actions) for firing it with `formbricks.track()`.

![The Survey Trigger section with the action attached](/images/surveys/best-practices/pmf-survey/trigger.webp)

<Tip>
  A code action is two halves that have to agree: the action you create here carries a **key**, and your
  app fires that same key with `formbricks.track("<key>")`. Nothing happens until both exist — the survey
  stays silent if the key never fires, and the call is a no-op if no action carries it. The no-code
  alternatives (Click, Page View, Exit Intent, 50% Scroll, Time on Page) are walked through on the
  [Feedback Box](/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box) page; the full reference is the
  [Actions manual](/surveys/website-app-surveys/actions).
</Tip>

## 3. Decide how often it may appear

Still on **Settings**, open **Visibility & Recontact**. Choose **Show only once**. Asking the same people to take the survey repeatedly is how you get a number that drifts for reasons unrelated to your product.

<Note>
  Recontact Options and the workspace-wide [Cooldown Period](/surveys/website-app-surveys/cooldown-period)
  are two separate gates and a survey has to pass both — Recontact Options first, then the cooldown. If
  your workspace shows at most one survey a week, this survey inherits that unless you set **Ignore
  Cooldown Period**.
</Note>

## 4. Publish

**Publish** puts it live. The widget picks up the change within a minute.

<Note>
  This is an app survey, so it needs the Formbricks widget running in your product. If you have not
  installed it yet, follow [the quickstart](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart) first.
</Note>
