The Feedback Box gives your users a direct channel to share what is on their mind, in two clicks, without leaving your app.

## Purpose

A low-friction channel catches the small frustrations that never make it into a support ticket. Because it is always available rather than scheduled, it also tells you *when* something broke.

## What you will build

A survey that opens whenever someone clicks a Feedback button on your website or web app, and that keeps working every time they click it — not once per person. There are two ways to build it:

- **[With Creatives](#with-creatives)** (self-hosted, Enterprise license that includes Creatives): Formbricks draws the button for you. Your team sets its label, look, position and pages in Formbricks, with no code beyond the snippet, and sees how often it was shown and clicked.
- **[Without Creatives](#without-creatives)** (Formbricks Cloud, Community Edition): you add a button to your own site and wire it to the survey with a click action.

<Note>
  Either way this is an app survey, so it needs the Formbricks widget running in your product. If you have not
  installed it yet, follow [the quickstart](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart) first.
</Note>

## 1. Start from the template

Open **Surveys**, click **New Survey** and choose **Choose a template**. Search for **Feedback Box**.

![The Feedback Box template in the template picker](/images/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box/template.webp)

Click the card to create the survey. The template opens with a "What is on your mind, boss?" choice — bug report or feature request — and branches into a follow-up question for each answer.

## 2. Make it yours

Edit the questions on the **Questions** tab. The wording matters more than it looks: "What is on your mind, boss?" sets a casual tone that suits some products and not others.

Keep it short. Every extra question costs you responses, and the value of a feedback box is that answering it is nearly free.

## With Creatives

A [feedback button](/surveys/creatives/feedback-button) opens its survey on every click by design (unless the survey is already on screen), so there is no trigger to set up and no recontact or cooldown setting to change.

### 3. Create the feedback button

Open **Creatives**, under **Ask**, click **New creative**, choose **Feedback button** and click **Continue**. On the **Content** tab:

- keep the label **Feedback**, or write your own, such as "Give feedback";
- pick an icon, or **None**;
- under **On click**, choose **Open a survey** and pick your Feedback Box survey. It reads **App survey · opens as a popup**.

If the survey is listed but can't be picked, it is **Restricted**: on self-hosted instances with [survey visibility](/surveys/general-features/visibility) turned on, new surveys start that way. Open **Collaborate** from the survey's row menu or editor and make it visible to the workspace; only its author and organization owners or managers can change this.

![A feedback button in the editor, opening the Feedback Box survey](/images/surveys/creatives/feedback-button/content.webp)

### 4. Place it

On the **Styling** tab, under **Position**, choose the edge and how far along it the button sits. The right edge, halfway down, is the default. On phones, the bottom edge often covers less content: switch **Position** to **Phone** and pick it there.

Leave **Add custom styles** off and the button matches the survey button in your [Look & Feel](/platform/features/styling-theme). Turn it on to set its own colors, font and size.

### 5. Choose where it shows

By default the button shows on every page that runs the snippet. To keep it off some pages, such as your checkout, open the **Settings** tab and add a **Show when** condition, for example **Current URL contains none of `/checkout`**. See [When It Shows](/surveys/creatives/when-it-shows).

### 6. Publish both

Publish the survey: it needs no trigger of its own, because the button opens it. Its **Survey Trigger** card lists the button under **Opened by**, and its **Display & Recontact** settings are disabled, since a click opens it unless it is already on screen.

Then click **Publish** on the feedback button. Returning visitors may take up to an hour to see it; new visitors see it within a few minutes.

On the button's summary page you can now follow its impressions, clicks and click rate, and every response records it as **Opened by creative**. See [Measure Performance](/surveys/creatives/measure-performance).

## Without Creatives

On Formbricks Cloud and the Community Edition, the button is part of your own site, and the survey opens on a click action.

### 3. Trigger it on your Feedback button

Add a button to your site, for example `<button id="feedback-button">Feedback</button>`. In the survey, open the **Settings** tab and find **Survey Trigger**. Click **Add action**, then **Capture new action**.

![The Add action dialog, on its Capture new action tab](/images/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box/action.webp)

Name the action, leave the type on **Click**, and identify your button in one of two ways:

- **Inner Text** — matches the visible text of the element that was clicked. Switch it on and enter `Feedback` if that is what your button says.
- **CSS Selector** — matches a class or id, such as `#feedback-button`. Use this when the label is translated, or when several elements share the text.

Switch on both and the click has to match both to count.

Once created, the action appears under the trigger:

![The Survey Trigger section with the Feedback action attached](/images/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box/trigger.webp)

<Tip>
  Reusing a button that already exists? **Select saved action** lists every action in the workspace, so
  you do not have to define the same click twice.
</Tip>

### 4. Let it open every time

By default a survey is shown to each person once. That is wrong for a feedback box — someone who reports a bug on Monday should still be able to request a feature on Tuesday.

Still on **Settings**, open **Display & Recontact** and choose **Keep showing while conditions match**.

![Recontact options, set to keep showing while conditions match](/images/surveys/best-practices/feedback-box/recontact.webp)

Then, in the same section, choose **Ignore Cooldown Period**.

This second setting is not optional if you want the box to open on every click. Recontact Options and the [Cooldown Period](/surveys/website-app-surveys/cooldown-period) are two separate gates and a survey has to pass both: Recontact Options are evaluated first, then the workspace-wide cooldown. So a box set to "keep showing" but left on **Use Cooldown Period** still goes quiet for a week after any other survey has appeared — which looks exactly like the feature being broken.

<Note>
  Ignoring the cooldown is the right call here and not in general. It is defensible because the user
  opened this survey deliberately; a survey that interrupts them uninvited should stay inside the
  workspace budget.
</Note>

### 5. Activate

**Activate** starts it. The widget picks up the change within a minute.

## Where the feedback goes

Responses land in the survey's **Responses** tab like any other. Two things worth setting up on day one:

- A [Workflow](/workflows/overview) that emails whoever triages feedback, so nobody has to remember to check.
- [Tags](/surveys/general-features/tags) on the responses, so "bug" and "feature request" stay separable once there are hundreds of them.
