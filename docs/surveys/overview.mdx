Formbricks surveys collect feedback from customers, users, and employees through link surveys, website surveys, and in-app surveys. Your agent creates, changes and reads them through [MCP](/platform/mcp/overview) and the [v3 API](/api-v3-reference/introduction). What respondents see stays the same: a hosted survey page, an embed, or a survey inside your website or app.

## Build

Ask your agent for the survey you need. It writes the survey document: blocks with questions, conditional logic, recall, variables, hidden fields, multiple languages, styling and Custom CSS. `generate_survey` drafts one from a prompt, `validate_survey` checks it, and `create_survey`, `patch_survey` and `edit_survey_blocks` save it. Nothing goes live until it is published.

## Distribute

Share a survey through a public link, embed it in your website, or trigger it in your web or mobile app. Display rules, such as triggers, recontact options, the Cooldown Period and show conditions, control who sees a survey and when.

## Analyze

Your agent reads responses (`list_responses`, `count_responses`, `get_response`), and surveys can feed [Unify Feedback](/unify-feedback/overview) so responses can be queried together with feedback from other sources. Stream responses out of Formbricks with [webhooks](/platform/webhooks).
