Creatives put a feedback button or a banner on the websites that run the Formbricks JavaScript SDK. Your team builds, schedules and pauses them in Formbricks, and each one opens a survey or a link on every click.

Creatives need an Enterprise license that includes them. If your license stops including Creatives, they are hidden from your sites and stay read-only in Formbricks until it does again. Impressions and clicks are written to the database by the background [job worker](/self-hosting/configuration/job-runner), which runs by default. No other configuration is needed.

Read the full guide: [Creatives](/surveys/creatives/overview).
