Creatives put a feedback button or a banner on the websites that run the Formbricks JavaScript SDK. Your team builds, schedules and pauses them in Formbricks, and each one opens a survey or a link on every click.

<Note>
  Creatives are part of the Formbricks [Enterprise Edition](/self-hosting/advanced/license) and need a license
  that includes them. They are not available on Formbricks Cloud. If your license stops including Creatives,
  they are hidden from your sites; you can still pause, close or delete them, and everything else is locked
  until the license includes Creatives again.
</Note>

Impressions and clicks are written to the database by the background [job worker](/self-hosting/configuration/job-runner), which runs by default. No other configuration is needed.

Read the full guide: [Creatives](/surveys/creatives/overview).
