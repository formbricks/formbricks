Unify Feedback consolidates feedback from across your stack into a single, queryable store. Survey responses and records pushed by your agent or your own systems land in the same model, so you can analyze them together.

## Why Unify Feedback

Most companies collect feedback in many places: surveys, support tickets, app store reviews, NPS tools, sales calls. Each lives in its own silo with its own schema. Unify Feedback normalizes all of these into **Feedback Records** grouped under **Feedback Datasets**, so they can be filtered, searched, counted and acted on together.

## How it works

1. **A Feedback Dataset** is a tenant-scoped bucket for related feedback (for example, "Product Feedback" or "Support 2026"), assigned to a workspace.
2. **Sources** bring data in: Formbricks survey responses, and records your agent or your systems push with `create_feedback_records` or the v3 API.
3. **Enrichment** adds sentiment, emotions, and a consolidated language to open-text feedback automatically.
4. **Topics & Subtopics** cluster open-text feedback with AI.
5. **Your agent queries it**: list and filter records, search by meaning, find similar feedback, and aggregate with `query_feedback`.
