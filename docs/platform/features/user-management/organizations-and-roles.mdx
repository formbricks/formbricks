Organization-level roles apply to all teams and Workspaces within your Formbricks organization. These roles provide broad permissions that determine what users can do across the entire organization.

<Note>
  Access Roles is a feature of the [Enterprise Edition](/self-hosting/advanced/license). In the **Community Edition** and on the **Free**
  and **Startup** plan in the Cloud you can invite unlimited organization members as `Owner`.
</Note>

## Role hierarchy

Here are the different access permissions, ranked from highest to lowest access:

1. **Owner** - Full organizational control
2. **Manager** - Management access with some restrictions
3. **Billing** - Billing and payment management only
4. **Member** - Basic access to assigned Workspaces
5. **Dashboard viewer** - Reads the dashboards of assigned Workspaces, nothing else

### Role Permissions and Privilege Escalation Prevention

To prevent privilege escalation, the following rules apply:

- **Owners** can:
  - Invite users as owners, managers, members, or Dashboard viewers
  - Assign roles up to owner level

- **Managers** can:
  - Invite users only as members or Dashboard viewers
  - Assign the member or Dashboard viewer role only, not manager or owner

- **Members** cannot:
  - Invite users
  - Assign roles

## Organization-level roles

All users and their organization-level roles are listed under **Settings → Organization → Teams**. The role dropdown beside each person is where you change one.

![The organization's member list, with a role beside each person](/images/platform/features/user-management/organizations-and-roles/members.webp)

Users can hold any of the following org-level roles:

### Owner
- Have full access to the organization, its data, and settings
- Can perform Team Admin actions without needing to join the team
- Can manage all aspects of the organization including billing, integrations, and member management

### Manager
- Have full management access to all teams and Workspaces
- Can manage the organization's membership (but can only invite or assign users as members)
- Can perform Team Admin actions without needing to join the team
- Cannot change other organization settings like billing or delete the organization

### Billing
- Can manage payment and compliance details in the organization
- Have access to billing settings and subscription management
- Cannot access other organizational data or settings

### Member
- Can view most data in the organization and act in the Workspaces they are members of
- Cannot create or join Workspaces on their own and need to be assigned by owners or managers
- Have limited permissions that depend on their Workspace-level access

### Dashboard viewer
- Can open the dashboards of the Workspaces their teams have access to, and nothing else in the organization
- Can use each dashboard's filters and date range, which only ever narrow what the dashboard's charts show
- Cannot see surveys, responses, contacts, Feedback Records, the chart builder, or organization and Workspace settings
- Cannot create, edit, or delete dashboards or charts

Read more in [Dashboard viewer](#dashboard-viewer-role) below.

## Dashboard viewer role

The Dashboard viewer role is for people who need to follow your feedback insights without access to the raw data
behind them, for example executives, other departments, or a client.

<Note>
  The Dashboard viewer role needs both **Access Control** and **Dashboards** on your plan. Until both are
  available, the role is not offered when you invite someone or change a role.
</Note>

### Which dashboards a Dashboard viewer sees

A Dashboard viewer sees the dashboards of every Workspace that one of their teams has access to. The team's
permission on the Workspace (Read, Read & Write, or Manage) makes no difference: a Dashboard viewer only ever reads
dashboards. To share dashboards with someone:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite them as a Dashboard viewer">
    Invite the person with the **Dashboard viewer** role, see [Invite members](/platform/features/user-management/invite-members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to a team">
    Add them to a team under **Settings → Organization → Teams**. A Dashboard viewer who is on no team sees no
    dashboards yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the team access to the Workspace">
    Give that team access to the Workspace whose dashboards they should see, see
    [Teams and roles](/platform/features/user-management/teams-and-roles).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What a Dashboard viewer can do

- Open the dashboards of their Workspaces. The navigation shows **Dashboards** and nothing else, and after signing
  in they land on the dashboards of the Workspace they used last.
- Pick values in a dashboard's filters and choose a date range. Filters only narrow what each chart's author saved;
  they never show data a chart leaves out. See [Dashboard filters](/unify-feedback/dashboards-charts#dashboard-filters).
- Switch between the organizations they belong to.
- Change their profile and notification settings, and see and revoke the apps they connected under
  **Account → Authorized apps**.
- Connect an MCP client or another app over OAuth. The app only gets to read the dashboards the Dashboard viewer
  can see, whatever else it asks for. The consent screen says so.

### What a Dashboard viewer can't do

- See surveys, responses, contacts, Feedback Records, Feedback Sources, or the chart builder.
- Create, edit, duplicate, or delete dashboards or charts.
- Open organization or Workspace settings, billing, API keys, or integrations.
- Read a chart outside a dashboard, or run their own chart queries through the API.

If a dashboard's charts show values of embedded data or answers, a Dashboard viewer can see those values in the
charts and in the filters' value lists. Choose which charts and filters you put on a dashboard with that in mind.

### When the plan changes

If your plan no longer includes Access Control or Dashboards, Dashboard viewers keep their role but see a
"Dashboards are not available on this plan" page instead of the dashboards. They are never turned into Members, so
they get no access they did not have. When the plan includes both again, their dashboards come back.

A Dashboard viewer who belongs to several organizations keeps every role they hold elsewhere. Restrictions on
connected apps apply only when someone is a Dashboard viewer in every organization they belong to; in an
organization where they hold another role, that role's permissions apply as usual.

## Best practices

- **Principle of least privilege**: Assign users the minimum role necessary for their responsibilities
- **Regular audits**: Periodically review organization members and their roles
- **Owner role**: Limit the number of owners to reduce security risk
- **Manager role**: Use for team leads who need to manage Workspaces but not organizational settings
- **Dashboard viewer role**: Use for people who only need to follow results, instead of giving them Member access
