Formbricks has AI-powered helpers that are controlled for the whole organization. AI is optional: Formbricks runs fully without it.

## AI Principles

1. **Always optional**: AI is disabled until your organization enables it. You can run Formbricks fully without AI.
2. **We separate AI**: We distinguish between:
   - **Smart Functionality**: helps teams build and operate faster, for example drafting a survey from a prompt with `generate_survey`.
   - **Data AI**: features that work directly with your feedback data (creating embeddings for Unify Feedback semantic search, enrichment, and topics).

### Privacy-first and self-hosted where possible

We prioritize self-hosted AI, especially for capabilities that process customer feedback data. Formbricks supports AI in self-hosted and on-premise environments, and we prefer open-weight models whenever feasible.

For Unify Feedback semantic search, we host the embeddings model ourselves. This means feedback data is not shared with third-party model providers for that capability, and your collected feedback/response data is never used as AI training input.

## Model Hosting Status

- **Current Smart Functionality model**: Gemini 3.5 Flash hosted on Google Cloud Platform in Germany.

- **Current Embeddings model**: Alibaba GTE embeddings hosted by Formbricks in Germany.

- **In progress**: evaluation of self-hosted Kimi 2.5 to replace Gemini 3.5 Flash for Smart Functionality.

## AI Features by Category

### Smart Functionality

- **Survey drafting**: `generate_survey` turns a prompt into a draft survey that your agent can review, edit and save.

### Data AI

- **Embeddings creation**: create embeddings for feedback records so they can be used for [semantic search](/unify-feedback/feedback-records), clustering, and retrieval.
- **[Enrichment](/unify-feedback/enrichment)** and **[Topics & Subtopics](/unify-feedback/topics-subtopics)**: sentiment, emotions, translation and clustering of open-text feedback.

## Permissions

Smart Functionality is switched on per organization and by the organization's plan. Self-hosters also connect a model to the instance; see [AI Features](/self-hosting/configuration/ai-features).
