Formbricks is an open-source (AGPLv3) feedback platform built to collect feedback from anyone (users, customers, or employees) on any platform, and to be operated by AI agents.

There is no survey editor and there are no results screens. You ask your agent, and it works through the Formbricks MCP server and API:

- **Standalone surveys (share via link):** Replace Google Forms, Typeform or any other link survey tool. Your agent creates the survey, and respondents open a hosted page.

- **Scalable website surveys:** Even if you have millions of website visitors, Formbricks lets you run well-timed and anonymously targeted [surveys on any public website](/surveys/website-app-surveys/quickstart).

- **Highly targeted app surveys:** Identify known users with Formbricks and enrich their profiles with attributes and specific actions. Build cohorts for highly targeted in-app surveys.

The survey platform is **mostly free, even for commercial use**. Over time, all survey features will stay part of the free Community Edition.

### Experience Management (XM)

- **What is XM?**
  Experience Management (XM) involves collecting, analysing, and reporting feedback from stakeholders (like customers or employees) to understand and improve their experience with your organisation.

- **Why XM Matters**
  Helping businesses, governments, and nonprofits understand their users' experiences leads to better services and happier people. Formbricks provides the data needed to make decisions that put people first.
